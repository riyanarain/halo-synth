HALO

Play light with your hands. A webcam, a synth, and a looper — nothing is sent anywhere.

Best in Chrome or Edge. Headphones recommended.

Am

Hands

Left hand — height picks the chord (ruler on the left). Open hand = louder, brighter pad. Tilt right for a 7th, left for sus2. Hold a fist to record / stop the loop.

Right hand — pinch thumb to index / middle / ring / pinky to play notes. Faster pinch = louder. Hand height = octave, tilt = brightness, move right = more echo and reverb.
